## What happens next

We confirm receipt and answer within one month (EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland) or 45 days (US states). Before we disclose or delete data we verify that the request comes from you, usually by e-mail to the address we have on file. You can use an authorised agent; we may ask for proof of authorisation. We do not charge for requests unless they are manifestly unfounded or excessive. If we decline a request, we explain why and how you can appeal or complain to a supervisory authority or your state's Attorney General.
