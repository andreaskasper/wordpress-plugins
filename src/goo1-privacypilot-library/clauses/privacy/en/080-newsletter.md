## Newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we use your e-mail address (and your name, if given) to send you news about our events. Where required, we confirm your subscription with a confirmation e-mail (double opt-in) and keep a record of it. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in every newsletter or by contacting us.
