## Minors

Teenagers aged 13 to 17 may register for some of our events, for example in junior divisions. Where the law requires it, we ask for the consent of a parent or guardian, and we use minors' information only to run the event. We do not sell or share minors' personal information for advertising. Our services are not directed at children under 13.
