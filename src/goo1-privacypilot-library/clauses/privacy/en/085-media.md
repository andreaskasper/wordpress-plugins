## Photos and videos at our events

We and photographers or videographers we commission take photos and videos at our events and publish some of them on our website, on social media and in videos of the event (for example competition videos). Dancing at a public event means you may appear in these recordings. We inform attendees about this at the event.

If you do not want to be shown in a published photo or video, tell us at {{entity.email}}; we will remove or blur it where reasonably possible.
