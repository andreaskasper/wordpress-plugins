## Your rights in the United States

Depending on the state you live in (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Texas, Oregon and other states with comprehensive privacy laws), you have some or all of the following rights:

- **Know and access**: to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, and to get a copy.
- **Correct** inaccurate personal information.
- **Delete** personal information we collected from you.
- **Portability**: to receive your information in a portable format.
- **Opt out** of the sale of personal information, of sharing for targeted advertising, and of profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.
- **Limit** the use of sensitive personal information, where we use it beyond what is necessary.
- **Non-discrimination**: we will not treat you differently because you exercise your rights.

To exercise your rights, use the form on our page {{link.choices}} or write to {{entity.email}}. We will confirm receipt and answer within 45 days; if we need more time (up to another 45 days), we will tell you why. To protect your information, we verify your identity before we disclose or delete data, usually by asking you to confirm details we already have. You may use an authorised agent; we may ask for proof of the agent's authority and verify your identity directly.

**Appeals**: if we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision or by choosing "Appeal" in the request form. We will answer your appeal within the period required by your state's law (usually 45 or 60 days). If you are not satisfied, you can contact your state's Attorney General.
