## Transfers outside the EU / EEA

Some of our service providers are located in the United States or other countries outside the EU/EEA and the UK, or we are located there ourselves. Where personal information is transferred to such countries, we rely on an adequacy decision of the European Commission (for the United States: the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for certified companies; for the UK: the UK Extension), or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Article 46(2)(c) GDPR) and, where needed, additional safeguards. You can ask us for a copy of these safeguards.
