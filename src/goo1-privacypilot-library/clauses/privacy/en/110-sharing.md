## Who we share personal information with

We share personal information only as described in this policy, in particular with:

- **Service providers** who host the website, process payments, send e-mails or provide the other services named above. They may use the information only to provide their services to us.
- **Authorities and advisers**, where the law requires it or where needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
- **A buyer or successor**, if our business or an event is transferred, under the same protection.
