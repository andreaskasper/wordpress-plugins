## How long we keep personal information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy. Registration and order data are kept for the duration of the event and as long as tax and commercial laws require (in many countries up to ten years for accounting records). Information we process on the basis of your consent is deleted when you withdraw your consent, unless another legal basis applies.
