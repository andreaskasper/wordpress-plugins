### Competitions and results

If you compete, your name, division, role, placements and the judges' scores are published on our website and on result pages of our scoring system. Results may also be passed to the organisations that maintain points registries for the dance style (for example the World Swing Dance Council, WSDC), because competing at a registry event means taking part in that points system. Published results are part of the event record and are generally kept permanently.
