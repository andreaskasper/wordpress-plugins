## What personal information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we collect the following categories of personal information:

- **Identifiers and contact details**, such as your name, e-mail address, postal address and phone number.
- **Internet and device information**, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, the pages you view and the date and time of your visit.
- **Approximate location**, derived from your IP address (country or region, not your exact position).
