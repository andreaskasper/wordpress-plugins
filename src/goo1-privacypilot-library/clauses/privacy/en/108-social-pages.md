## Our pages on social networks

We run pages on social networks such as Facebook, Instagram or YouTube. When you visit or interact with these pages, the network processes your data under its own privacy policy. For page statistics provided by the network, the network and we may be jointly responsible; for Facebook and Instagram, Meta has published an agreement on this joint responsibility. You can exercise your rights with the network or with us.
