## Your rights in the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

Under the GDPR (and the UK GDPR and Swiss data protection law) you have the right to:

- access the personal information we hold about you (Article 15 GDPR),
- have incorrect information corrected (Article 16),
- have your information deleted (Article 17),
- have processing restricted (Article 18),
- receive your information in a common machine-readable format (Article 20),
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interests, for reasons arising from your situation, and to direct marketing at any time (Article 21),
- withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Article 7(3)).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live or work or where the alleged infringement took place.
