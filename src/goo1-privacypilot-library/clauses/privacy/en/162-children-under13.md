## Children

Children, including children under 13, may take part in some of our events, for example in junior divisions. We collect children's personal information only with verifiable consent from a parent or guardian, only the information needed to run the event, and we do not use it for advertising or sell or share it. Parents and guardians can review their child's information, ask us to delete it and refuse further collection at any time by contacting us at {{entity.email}}.
