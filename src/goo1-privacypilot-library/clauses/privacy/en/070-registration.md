## Event registration and tickets

When you register for an event or buy a pass or ticket, we process the information needed to manage your registration: your contact details, the options you choose, payment status and, where needed, information you give us voluntarily (for example a dance partner or accommodation request). We use this information to run the event, to contact you about it and to meet our accounting and tax obligations.
