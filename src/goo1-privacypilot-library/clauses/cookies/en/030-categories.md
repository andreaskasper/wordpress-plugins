## Categories

- **Necessary**: needed for the website to work, for security and to remember your privacy choices. These cannot be switched off.
- **Functional**: extra functions, such as spam protection for forms.
- **Statistics**: help us understand how the website is used.
- **Marketing**: measure and personalise advertising.
- **External media**: content from other platforms, such as videos and maps.
